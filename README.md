## `epoch 01 — THE VOID`

Every tutorial builds a sentiment classifier on IMDB. Every portfolio has the same Titanic notebook. I looked at that and felt something close to physical pain.

Meanwhile: **Pashto.** Forty million speakers. Poetry older than most nations. And on HuggingFace? Silence. Broken tokenizers gagging on `ښ ږ ډ ړ ټ ڼ ې ۍ`. Datasets that were three scraped news sites in a trench coat. No benchmark. No baseline. No one coming.

So I stopped waiting for someone.
